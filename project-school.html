SCHOOL

A school website that helps families explore programmes, school life and admission steps.

Visit the school website ↗
Role and brief

Information for families

I designed and built a school website for parents comparing early-years and primary options. Programme pages make the age ranges clear: Creche, Nursery and Primary.

Technical choices

Separate routes for each school stage

The multi-page front end uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Separate pages cover the school, Creche, Nursery, Primary, Student Life, Gallery, Contact and Apply, so families can go directly to the information they need.

What changed

A clearer path from discovery to contact

Families can move from an age-specific programme to an admission enquiry, visit booking or WhatsApp conversation. The live site is linked above; no traffic or admissions results have been supplied.
